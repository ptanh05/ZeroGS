# Abstract

3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has emerged as a premier paradigm for real-time novel-view synthesis, yet its heuristic densification mechanics induce unconstrained primitive proliferation, routinely triggering catastrophic Out-Of-Memory (OOM) aborts on commodity hardware with 8 GB of VRAM. Existing memory-reduction strategies impose rigid population caps or aggressive periodic opacity culling, but these mechanisms inadvertently trigger what we identify as the **Over-Pruning Dilemma**: low-opacity primitives necessary for developing high-frequency foliage and fine surface geometry are repeatedly purged before gradient maturity, imposing severe reconstruction penalties exceeding $11\text{ dB}$.

To resolve this fundamental tension between GPU memory boundedness and visual fidelity, we introduce **PV-SRA-3DGS** (Peak-VRAM-Constrained Spatial Resource Allocation for 3D Gaussian Splatting). PV-SRA-3DGS replaces heuristic culling with a principled, four-tier resource allocation framework:
1. **Calibrated Affine Cost Modeling:** An analytical GPU memory formulation that rigorously decouples persistent storage ($944\text{ bytes}$ per primitive including Adam optimizer states) from transient mutation spikes ($1{,}137\text{ bytes}$ per clone, $2{,}429\text{ bytes}$ per split).
2. **Dynamic Headroom-Aware Inflow Governor:** An adaptive admission controller that dynamically scales per-cycle primitive admission between $5{,}000$ and $15{,}000$ candidates based on real-time usable VRAM headroom, throttling candidate admission during memory compression without stalling optimization.
3. **Marginal Knapsack Spatial Allocator:** A dual-pool greedy knapsack scheduler that prioritizes spatial primitives by marginal utility per byte ($\lambda = \Delta \text{Demand} / \text{Byte}$) subject to a $40\%$ split quota, resolving volumetric detail without redundant cloning.
4. **Preservation-Centric Lifecycle:** A decoupled pruning architecture that synchronizes opacity culling with primitive maturation, completely eliminating premature eviction during densification intervals.

We evaluate PV-SRA-3DGS through 23 controlled physical hardware benchmarks on an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Laptop GPU (8,151 MiB VRAM) across the Mip-NeRF 360 benchmark. Our framework achieves **zero OOM exceptions across all 30,000-iteration trials**, maintaining peak device VRAM strictly between $6{,}906$ and $7{,}570\text{ MiB}$. On `bonsai`, PV-SRA-3DGS reaches **$31.83 \pm 0.48\text{ dB}$ PSNR**, outperforming unconstrained Vanilla 3DGS ($29.59\text{ dB}$) by **$+2.24\text{ dB}$** while utilizing **$40.2\%$ fewer Gaussians** ($738\text{K}$ vs $1.24\text{M}$) and establishing an **$+11.0850 \pm 2.3383\text{ dB}$ paired improvement** over bounded over-pruning baselines ($p = 0.01451$, Cohen's $d = 4.7406$). Cross-scene evaluations confirm robust generalizability on unbounded outdoor environments (`garden`: $+6.2621\text{ dB}$ PSNR, $+93.8\%$ SSIM) and intricate foliage (`flowers`: $+2.9422\text{ dB}$ PSNR, $+53.1\%$ SSIM) under extreme candidate generation ($8.91\text{M}$ requests).
